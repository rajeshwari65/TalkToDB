Returns Policy — Effective January 2026

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery provided they are unused,
in their original packaging, and accompanied by the original order
confirmation. Items marked as "final sale" at the time of purchase are not
eligible for return.

Once a return is received and inspected, the customer is notified of
approval or rejection within 3 business days. Approved returns are
processed as a refund under the terms of the Refund Policy, or as a store
credit if the customer requests it instead.

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is
due to a defect, damage in transit, or an error in fulfillment on the
company's part, in which case return shipping is free and a prepaid label
is provided.
