Refund Policy — Effective January 2026

Customers are eligible for a full refund if two conditions are both met: the
refund request is made within 30 days of the order date, and the order's
current status is not already "refunded". Orders with status "pending" are
not eligible for a refund until the order is marked "completed" — a pending
order must first complete or be cancelled before any refund can be processed.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days
of approval. Where store credit is offered as an alternative to a cash
refund, it is issued within 24 hours of the customer's request instead.

Orders flagged for fraud review, or associated with a violation of the
company's Terms of Service, are not eligible for a refund under any
circumstances, regardless of the 30-day window.

Partial refunds may be issued at the discretion of the support team when
only part of an order is being returned; in these cases the refunded amount
is prorated based on the returned items' share of the total order amount.

Customers who signed up for a subscription plan follow a separate
cancellation policy and are not covered by this refund policy for
subscription charges.
