Shipping Policy — Effective January 2026

Standard shipping takes 5 to 8 business days from the date an order is
marked "completed". Expedited shipping, where selected at checkout, takes 2
to 3 business days and incurs an additional fee shown at checkout.

Orders are shipped only to addresses within regions the company currently
serves; international shipping outside these regions is not currently
supported.

A tracking number is emailed to the customer once the order leaves the
warehouse. Delivery delays beyond the stated window should be reported to
support so a replacement or refund can be considered under the Refund
Policy.

Shipping costs are non-refundable once an order has shipped, except in cases
where the delay or damage is attributable to the company's shipping partner.
